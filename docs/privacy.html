This policy explains what information The Hot Seat Discord bot ("the Bot," "we," "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect only what the Bot needs to run its games, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

The Bot works through Discord slash commands and buttons. Each time you use one, Discord sends the Bot basic information about the interaction (such as your user ID, username, and the server and channel it happened in). We store only the items below.

InformationWhen it's storedWhy
Server ID and server settings (18+ on/off, selected 18+ channel IDs, admin role ID, default rating mode, voter names on/off)When the Bot is first used in a server, or an admin changes /settingsTo apply each server's game settings

We do not read or store your messages, DMs, or voice chat. We don't request Discord's privileged intents (such as message content, member lists, or presence), and we don't collect email addresses, payment details, or location.

2. Credit on cards

When you submit a prompt, credit is off by default. If you turn it on, your Discord display name appears on that card ("Submitted by …") in every server that uses the Bot.

3. Who can see your information

  • Our review team (the developer and approved volunteer reviewers) sees submissions and reports in a private channel in our support server. This includes the submitter's or reporter's Discord name and ID.
  • Players see prompts, vote totals, who voted each way (unless the server turned voter names off), and the submitter's name only if credit was turned on.
  • Service providers that run the Bot: Discord (the platform), Cloudflare (hosting), and Neon (database hosting in the United States). They process data only to provide their services to us.

We don't sell, rent, or share your data for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

4. How long we keep it

  • Votes: deleted after 14 days.
  • Server settings and seen-prompt history: kept while the server uses the Bot. Ask us and we'll delete them.
  • Submissions and reports: kept as part of the deck's moderation history, including rejected or removed prompts, so we can block duplicates and repeat abuse.
  • Error logs kept by our hosting provider are short-term and used only for troubleshooting.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we have linked to your Discord user ID or server,
  • delete your votes, reports, and trusted status,
  • remove your name and user ID from your submissions (approved prompts may stay in the deck without your name), or
  • delete a server's settings and history (server owners or admins).

To make a request, contact us through the support server (see below). We may ask you to confirm your Discord account first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

6. Age and 18+ content

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement to use the Bot. 18+ prompts are only shown when a server admin enables them and the channel or server is marked Age-Restricted in Discord. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Paid features

The Bot is free today. If we add paid features later, payments will be handled by Discord or another payment provider. We would receive only whether a server or user has an active subscription, never your card details.

8. Security

Data is stored with reputable providers using encrypted connections, and access is limited to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the change in the support server. If you keep using the Bot after a change, you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions or requests? Join the support server: The Hot Seat support server.
